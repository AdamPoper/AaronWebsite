Our Method

  • First, we will conduct a predictive site survey with electronic copies of your building's floor plan to provide a quote.
  • Next, we will perform a passive on-site survey capturing data of the current RF environment. This will help us adjust our predictive site survey and quote if needed.
  • Then, we will install the agreed upon networking equipment and perform an active site survey. During the active site survey, coverage and throughput of the new WLAN will be validated.
  • Last, you can test your organization's devices and applications. We will not be hands off until everything is online and functional.
